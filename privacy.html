Lateralis privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Lateralis is a private, personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data

When the owner signs in with Google, Lateralis can read and change, at the owner's request, his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Docs, Google Contacts and Google Tasks. It does this only to carry out what the owner asks.

  • Google data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's phone. Lateralis has no server of its own and does not store, sell or share Google data.
  • To answer a request, the relevant part may be sent to the AI service the owner uses (Anthropic) for that request only.
  • Lateralis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the phone's keychain. Signing out in Lateralis, or removing access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, ends all access.
